### 1. If the main contribution is **robotics / navigation / embodied AI**

* IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) — very good fit if we emphasize navigation, exploration, and embodied learning.
* IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) — harder, higher prestige.
* Robotics and Autonomous Systems
* Autonomous Robots
* IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) — excellent if we can make the experiments clean and concise.

---

### 2. If the main contribution is **neuroevolution / spiking neural networks**

* Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference (GECCO)
* Neural Networks
* Evolutionary Computation
* Frontiers in Neurorobotics

This works if the paper is really about comparing random / Lévy / uniform / evolved NN / spiking policies and showing adaptation advantages.

---

### 3. If the pitch is **self-grounded cognition / artificial life**

The README language (“objecthood from pure sensorimotor experience”, “bootstrap internal representations”, “self-sustaining computational systems”) leans heavily here.

Best venues:

* Conference on Artificial Life (ALIFE)
* Artificial Life
* Adaptive Behavior

Honestly, this seems philosophically closest to the “Life on Silicon” direction.
